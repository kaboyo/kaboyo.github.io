Combined genomic and phenomic analyses reveal multifunctionality of Paenibacillus polymyxa K16 for plant nutrition, growth, and health

A combined genomic and phenomic workflow characterises Paenibacillus polymyxa K16 as a multifunctional bioinoculant strain.

Citation. Vitali, F.*, Trzciński, P., Manfredini, A., Olimi, E., Bigiotti, G., Ptaszek, M., Sas-Paszt, L., Bickel, S., Berg, G., Canfora, L., Malusà, E., & Mocali, S. (2025). Combined genomic and phenomic analyses reveal multifunctionality of Paenibacillus polymyxa K16 for plant nutrition, growth, and health. Scientific Reports, 15, 33487. doi:10.1038/s41598-025-15862-4

Expedito Olimi’s role. Co-author.

Summary

Beneficial microorganisms are an alternative to chemicals in agriculture, but the selection of bioproducts and their efficacy in the field need to improve. This paper develops a combined genomic and phenomic approach for characterising bioproducts and demonstrates it on a real strain, Paenibacillus polymyxa K16. The genome carried functional genes for plant nutrition, growth and health, and the phenomic analysis confirmed that the associated metabolic pathways are expressed. The authors propose the approach for routine use in screening programmes.

Key findings

  • The genome contained genes supporting plant nutrition (for example nitrate conversion into ammonia, phosphorus solubilisation, siderophore production), plant growth (plant hormones) and plant health (antagonism to plant pathogens).
  • Phenomic analysis showed varied carbon, nitrogen, phosphate and sulfur utilisation patterns and confirmed expression of the associated pathways.
  • Combining both data types gives more value than either technology alone and supports data-driven decisions in bioproduct optimisation.

Methods

Genome sequencing and functional gene mining; phenomic profiling of nutrient utilisation.
