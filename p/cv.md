## Academic and research positions

**From 2027** · Horizon MSCA Cofund REACH-UPPA Postdoctoral Fellow<br>
Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour (UPPA), France. Cross-kingdom communication between plants and microbes: deciphering the role of Microbiome (M) genes for promoting plant health.

**Jan 2026 – present** · Erwin Schrödinger Fellow, Principal Investigator<br>
Institute of Environmental Biotechnology, TU Graz, Austria. Return phase: host genetic and chemical drivers of seed microbiome assembly; strain isolation and synthetic community design; supervision of BSc and MSc researchers.

**Jan 2024 – Dec 2025** · Erwin Schrödinger Fellow, Principal Investigator<br>
School of Biological Sciences, University of Southampton, United Kingdom. Abroad phase: host genetic and chemical drivers of seed microbiome assembly; proof-of-principle datasets on the M gene framework and its application in nematode protection.

**Jun 2023 – Dec 2023** · Postdoctoral Researcher<br>
Institute of Environmental Biotechnology, TU Graz, Austria. H2020 EXCALIBUR: sampling and analysis of microbiome and soil biota datasets across European horticultural farms.

**Oct 2019 – May 2023** · Doctoral Researcher<br>
TU Graz, Austria. H2020 EXCALIBUR work package contributor (Grant 817946; 16 partners, 10 countries).

**2018 – 2019** · MSc Research Student<br>
Ghent University, Belgium. Rice and *Meloidogyne graminicola* pathosystem; in planta nematode infection assays with CRISPR-Cas9 engineered rice lines.

**2018** · Research Intern<br>
Real IPM | Biobest Group, Nairobi, Kenya. Yeast-based biocontrol (*Hyphopichia* spp.) against *Botrytis cinerea* in commercial rose production.

**2016 – 2017** · Graduate Research Assistant<br>
International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA), Uganda. Nematode management and healthy seedling systems for peri-urban farming communities.

**2015 – 2016** · Apprentice<br>
National Banana Research Programme, Uganda. Molecular characterisation of transgenic banana lines for resistance to banana bacterial wilt.
