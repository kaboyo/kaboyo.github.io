Combining phenomics and genomics for bioproduct development and use in agriculture

Phenotypic and genomic characterisation of Paenibacillus polymyxa K16 as an example dossier for a new bio-product.

Citation. Vitali, F., Trzciński, P., Manfredini, A., Olimi, E., et al. (2025). Combining phenomics and genomics for bioproduct development and use in agriculture. Acta Horticulturae, 1427, 24. doi:10.17660/ActaHortic.2025.1427.24

Expedito Olimi’s role. Co-author.

Summary

Better knowledge of the biological processes behind the field efficacy of microbial biostimulants is needed to promote their use. This contribution presents the bio-fertiliser and bio-pesticide potential of Paenibacillus polymyxa strain K16, isolated from tomato roots, as an example of the dossier needed to characterise a novel bio-product before it reaches the market. The strain was characterised phenotypically and its genome was sequenced, and potential functions were inferred with different mining methods.

Key findings

  • Strain K16 has various potential biofertiliser and biopesticide functions.
  • Phenomic analysis showed strong utilisation of carbon sources, with the highest median activity on phosphate and sulfur compounds.
  • Genes and measured metabolic activities were connected through metabolic pathway analysis.

Methods

Phenotypic characterisation, genome sequencing, functional mining, metabolic pathway analysis.

Keywords

Paenibacillus polymyxa, bioproducts, biostimulants, phenomics, genomics
